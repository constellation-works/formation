Record a decision under your authority, or as the admiral from a terminal, and mail the asker

Usage: formation request settle [OPTIONS] --option <LABEL> <REQUEST>

Arguments:
  <REQUEST>  Request identifier

Options:
      --option <LABEL>    Label of the option you choose
      --room <NAME|PATH>  Room name or directory path (default: $FORMATION_ROOM or session file)
      --as <SEAT>         Joined seat to act as (default: this session's seat, or admiral at a terminal)
      --note <TEXT>       Explain the decision
      --admiral           Record the admiral's chat answer from a joined seat
      --json              Print machine-readable JSON
      --source <TEXT>     Where the admiral gave the answer (required with --admiral)
  -h, --help              Print help
