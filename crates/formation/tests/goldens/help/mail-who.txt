List every joined seat, not only those seen lately: undelivered, unacknowledged, held, seen

Usage: formation mail who [OPTIONS]

Options:
      --room <NAME|PATH>  Room name or directory path (default: $FORMATION_ROOM or session file)
      --as <SEAT>         Seat name for this command (default: seat recorded for this session)
      --json              Print JSON, overriding $FORMATION_FORMAT (auto, human or json)
  -h, --help              Print help
