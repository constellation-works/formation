Revoke an active grant or withhold by identifier

Usage: formation grant revoke [OPTIONS] <GRANT>

Arguments:
  <GRANT>  Grant identifier printed by `grant add`, `grant withhold` or `grant list`

Options:
      --room <NAME|PATH>  Room name or directory path (default: $FORMATION_ROOM or session file)
      --as <SEAT>         Seat name for this command (default: seat recorded for this session, or none outside one)
  -h, --help              Print help

Every joined seat whose effective authority changes receives one mail envelope.

Example:
  formation grant revoke 1
